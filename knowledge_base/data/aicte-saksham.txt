AICTE Saksham Scholarship for Specially-Abled Students — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Saksham — Scholarship for Specially-Abled Students for pursuing Technical Education.
Publisher: All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of Education, Government of India.
Official URL: https://www.aicte-india.org/sites/default/files/policy/Saksham.pdf

Eligibility (as published by AICTE):
- The candidate should be a specially-abled student with at least 40% disability
  (certificate issued by a competent medical authority).
- Admitted to the FIRST year of a Diploma or Degree programme in an AICTE-approved
  institution.
- Family income from all sources should not exceed Rs. 8 lakh per annum.
- Maximum two specially-abled children per family are eligible.

Scholarship value:
- Tuition fee of Rs. 30,000 per annum, OR actual tuition fee paid, whichever is less.
- Rs. 2,000 per month for 10 months as incidental charges (Rs. 20,000 per annum).

Documents typically required:
- Disability certificate with 40% or more disability.
- Class 10 / 12 marksheet.
- Admission proof at an AICTE-approved institution.
- Income certificate.
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked preferred).
- Bonafide certificate.

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in).
2. Choose "Saksham" under AICTE schemes.
3. Provide disability, academic, income and bank details.
4. Upload documents and submit for institution verification.
5. AICTE scrutinises; disbursal is via DBT to the student's account.

Notes:
- Annual deadline is published on the NSP; confirm before applying.
- Confirm the current year's exact disability-document format with AICTE.
