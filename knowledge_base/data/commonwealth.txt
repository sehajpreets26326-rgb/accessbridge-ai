Commonwealth Scholarships (UK) — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Commonwealth Scholarships and Fellowships (UK).
Publisher: Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK (CSC), funded by the
UK Government through the FCDO.
Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/

What it is:
- Fully-funded master's and PhD scholarships for citizens of Commonwealth
  developing countries to study in the UK.

Eligibility (typical for Commonwealth Master's Scholarship):
- Citizen of an eligible Commonwealth country (or a British protected person,
  refugee, or stateless person).
- Hold at least an upper second-class (2:1) UK undergraduate honours degree or
  equivalent.
- Be unable to afford to study in the UK without this scholarship.
- Apply to a UK university course that is supported by the CSC.

Award value:
- Full tuition fees.
- Living allowance (stipend) at the UK rate.
- Return airfare to the UK.
- Arrival allowance, thesis grant (where applicable).
- Travel grant for study-related travel within the UK.

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply through a nominating body in the home country (e.g. the Ministry of
   Education / UGC in India) OR directly via the CSC online application system,
   depending on the route published for the year.
2. Submit application form, academic transcripts, references, and a study plan.
3. Apply to UK universities and obtain at least one admission offer by the
   stated deadline.
4. Shortlisting and selection by the CSC.
5. Final selection; travel to the UK in September/October.

Notes:
- Confirm exact deadlines, eligible courses and nominating-body route for the
  current year on the CSC website.
- Commonwealth Split-site, PhD and Distance Learning scholarships have
  different eligibility — choose the right scheme.
