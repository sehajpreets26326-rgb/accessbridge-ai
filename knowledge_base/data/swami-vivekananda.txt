Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (SVMCM).
Publisher: Government of West Bengal (Higher Education Department).
Official URL: https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in/

Eligibility (typical):
- Domicile of West Bengal.
- Studying in Class XI, XII, or undergraduate / postgraduate / diploma courses
  (including medical, engineering, para-medical) in an institution within West
  Bengal.
- At least 75% marks in the last qualifying examination (Madhyamik or
  equivalent for HS; HS for UG; UG for PG).
- Annual family income from all sources should not exceed Rs. 2.5 lakh.

Scholarship value (typical ranges):
- HS / UG (arts / commerce): Rs. 1,000/month for 10 months.
- UG (science / engineering / medical / professional): up to Rs. 1,500–5,000/month
  depending on course level.
- PG: Rs. 2,000–5,000/month depending on course level.

Documents commonly required:
- Marksheet of the last qualifying exam (with 75%+).
- Income certificate (issued by competent authority, recent).
- Domicile / residential certificate of West Bengal.
- Bonafide certificate from the current institution.
- Bank account details (in the student's name, preferably at a public-sector bank).
- Recent passport photograph.

Application steps (typical):
1. Register on the SVMCM portal (svmcm.wbhed.gov.in) during the open window.
2. Fill academic, institution, income and bank details.
3. Upload documents.
4. Institution verification.
5. Approval and disbursal via DBT to the student's bank account.

Notes:
- Confirm the current-year income limit, mark threshold and deadline on the
  official SVMCM portal before applying.
