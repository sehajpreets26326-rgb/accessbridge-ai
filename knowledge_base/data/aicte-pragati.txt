AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Pragati — Scholarship for Girl Students for pursuing Technical Education.
Publisher: All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of Education, Government of India.
Official URL: https://www.aicte-india.org/sites/default/files/policy/Pragati.pdf

Eligibility (as published by AICTE):
- The candidate should be a girl student.
- She should be admitted to the FIRST year of a Diploma or Degree programme in
  an AICTE-approved institution.
- The family income from all sources should not exceed Rs. 8 lakh per annum
  during the preceding financial year.
- Maximum TWO girl children per family are eligible.
- The candidate must not be in receipt of any other scholarship of equal or
  higher amount for the same course.

Scholarship value:
- Tuition fee of Rs. 30,000 per annum, OR actual tuition fee paid, whichever is less.
- Rs. 2,000 per month for 10 months in an academic year towards incidental
  charges (a total of Rs. 20,000 per annum).

Documents typically required:
- Class 10 / 12 marksheet (as applicable).
- Diploma / degree admission proof at an AICTE-approved institute.
- Income certificate issued by a competent authority (not older than the
  prescribed validity).
- Category / caste certificate where applicable.
- Bank account details of the student (Aadhaar-linked bank account preferred).
- Bonafide certificate from the institution.
- AICTE Pragati application confirmation.

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) when the
   scheme window is open.
2. Select "Pragati" under AICTE schemes.
3. Fill in academic, institution and family income details.
4. Upload documents and submit.
5. Institution verifies the application online.
6. AICTE scrutinises and approves; scholarship is disbursed directly to the
   student's bank account via DBT.

Notes:
- Deadlines vary each year and are announced on scholarships.gov.in.
- Always confirm the current year's deadline and document list on the official
  National Scholarship Portal before applying.
