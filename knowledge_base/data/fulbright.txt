Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowships — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowships (for Indian citizens).
Publisher: United States-India Educational Foundation (USIEF), funded jointly by
the Governments of India and the United States.
Official URL: https://www.usief.org.in/Fellowships-for-Indian-Citizens.aspx

Eligibility (typical):
- Indian citizen living in India at the time of application.
- Hold at least a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 55% marks (or equivalent).
- Have at least three years of professional / work experience relevant to the
  proposed field of study.
- Apply to a US master's programme in an eligible field (e.g. public
  administration, public health, urban planning, business administration,
  economics, journalism, environmental science, higher education administration).
- Demonstrate leadership, community service and a clear plan to return to India.

Award value:
- J-1 visa support.
- Round-trip economy airfare from India to the US.
- Tuition and fees for the master's programme.
- Monthly stipend for living expenses.
- Accident and sickness coverage as per US Government guidelines.
- Modest allowances for books and settling-in.

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply online through the USIEF application portal during the annual window
   (typically May–July for the following academic year).
2. Submit the application form, statement of purpose, resume, academic
   transcripts, three letters of reference, and standardised test scores
   (GRE/GMAT and TOEFL/IELTS) as required.
3. First-stage review and shortlisting by USIEF.
4. Interview of shortlisted candidates.
5. Recommended candidates apply to US universities.
6. Final selection after university admission is confirmed.

Notes:
- Confirm current-year deadlines and eligible fields on the USIEF website.
- Some Fulbright-Nehru awards (Doctoral, Postdoctoral, Distinguished) have
  different eligibility — pick the right one.
