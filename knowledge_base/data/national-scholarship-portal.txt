National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — official overview (bundled excerpt)

Portal: National Scholarship Portal.
Publisher: Government of India, Ministry of Education (and partner ministries).
Official URL: https://scholarships.gov.in/

What it is:
- NSP is the single online platform for central and state scholarship schemes in
  India. It covers pre-matric, post-matric, merit-cum-means and several
  scheme-specific scholarships (AICTE Pragati & Saksham, UGC Ishan Uday, Post-
  Matric SC/ST/OBC, and others).

Eligibility (general pattern):
- Indian nationals studying in recognised institutions.
- Specific eligibility (income, category, course level) depends on each scheme.
- Family income limit is typically Rs. 1 lakh to Rs. 8 lakh per annum depending
  on the scheme.

Documents commonly required:
- Aadhaar / valid ID.
- Income certificate (issued by competent authority).
- Caste / category certificate where applicable.
- Previous qualifying exam marksheet.
- Bonafide certificate from the current institution.
- Bank passbook copy (Aadhaar-linked bank account preferred).
- Recent passport-size photograph.

Application steps (typical):
1. Register on scholarships.gov.in with an OTP-verified mobile and email.
2. Complete the OTR (One Time Registration).
3. Choose the relevant scholarship scheme and year.
4. Fill academic, institution and bank details.
5. Upload documents and submit before the deadline.
6. Institution and district/state officer verify online.
7. Approval and DBT disbursal to the student's account.

Notes:
- Each scheme has its own deadline window; check the portal every year.
- The portal also shows scheme status, application status and disbursal history.
- Always verify the current year's scheme list and income criteria on NSP.
