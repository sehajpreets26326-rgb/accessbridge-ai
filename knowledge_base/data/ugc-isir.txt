UGC Ishan UDAY Special Scholarship for North Eastern Region — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Ishan UDAY — Special Scholarship Scheme for students of North Eastern Region.
Publisher: University Grants Commission (UGC), Ministry of Education, Government of India.
Official URL: https://www.ugc.gov.in/scholarshipschemes

Eligibility (as published by UGC):
- Student must be a domicile of any of the eight North Eastern states:
  Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura.
- First-year admission to a recognised degree course in a university / college
  located in India (general / professional / technical / medical courses).
- Family income from all sources must be less than Rs. 4.5 lakh per annum.
- Transgender students are eligible (per UGC's inclusion norms).
- Students already receiving any other scholarship of equal or higher value for
  the same course are not eligible.

Scholarship value:
- Rs. 5,400 per month for general degree courses (Rs. 54,000 per year for 10 months).
- Rs. 7,800 per month for technical / professional / medical courses
  (Rs. 78,000 per year for 10 months).

Documents commonly required:
- NER domicile / residence certificate.
- Class 12 marksheet and admission proof.
- Income certificate (issued by competent authority).
- Bank account details (preferably in the student's name).
- Aadhaar / valid photo ID.
- Bonafide certificate from the institution.

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) — Ishan UDAY
   appears under UGC schemes.
2. Register / log in with OTR.
3. Fill NER domicile, course, institution, income and bank details.
4. Upload documents.
5. Institution verifies the application online.
6. UGC scrutinises; disbursal is via DBT.

Notes:
- Annual deadline is published on the NSP. Confirm before applying.
- Verify current-year income and domicile criteria on the official UGC page.
