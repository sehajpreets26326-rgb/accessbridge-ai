Chevening Scholarships — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Chevening Scholarships (UK Government's international awards).
Publisher: UK Government / Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO),
administered by the Chevening Secretariat.
Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/

What it is:
- Fully-funded master's (one-year) scholarships in the UK for outstanding
  emerging leaders from around the world, including India.

Eligibility (typical):
- Citizen of an eligible Chevening country (India is eligible).
- Hold an undergraduate degree of a standard equivalent to a UK upper
  second-class (2:1) honours degree.
- At least two years (2,800 hours) of work experience by the application
  deadline (work, internship, voluntary, paid or unpaid counts).
- Meet the English language requirement (IELTS / TOEFL iBT / etc.).
- Apply to at least three eligible UK master's courses and receive at least one
  unconditional offer by the stated deadline.
- Not be a UK/British citizen or dual-national, and not have previously studied
  in the UK on a UK Government-funded scholarship.

Award value:
- Full tuition fees.
- Monthly living stipend.
- Return economy flights to and from the UK.
- Arrival and departure allowances.
- Travel grant for Chevening events.
- Thesis / dissertation grant (where applicable).

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply online at chevening.org/scholarships/applications during the annual
   window (typically August–early November).
2. Complete the eligibility quiz, biographical and statement-section questions.
3. Choose three eligible UK master's programmes.
4. Submit two references and the English-language test score by the deadline.
5. Shortlisting, interview at the British Embassy/High Commission.
6. Conditional selection, then unconditional offer from a UK university by July.
7. Final selection and travel to the UK in September.

Notes:
- Confirm exact deadline and eligibility nuances for the current year on
  chevening.org. Indian applicants apply through the same global portal.
