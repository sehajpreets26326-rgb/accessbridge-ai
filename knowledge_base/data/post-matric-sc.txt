Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students — official scheme details (bundled excerpt)

Scheme: Post-Matric Scholarship (PMS) for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students.
Publisher: Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India.
Official URL: https://socialjustice.gov.in/scholarship-schemes

Eligibility (as published):
- Indian national belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC).
- Studying at post-matriculation level (Class 11 onwards) in a recognised
  institution in India.
- Family income from all sources should not exceed Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum
  (verify current-year limit on the official portal).

Compensation:
- Maintenance allowance (hostel / day-scholar, group A–H based on course level).
- Reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable fees.
- Study tour charges, thesis typing/printing allowance where applicable.
- Book allowance for students pursuing correspondence / distance courses.

Documents commonly required:
- Caste certificate (SC) issued by competent authority.
- Income certificate.
- Marksheet of the last qualifying exam.
- Bonafide certificate for the current year.
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked preferred).
- Fee receipt / fee structure from the institution.

Application steps (typical):
1. Apply through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in).
2. Select Post-Matric SC scheme.
3. Provide caste, income, course, institution and bank details.
4. Upload documents; submit before the deadline.
5. Institution verification, then state/UT officer verification.
6. Disbursal via DBT.

Notes:
- Confirm the current-year income limit and renewal process on the portal.
- Renewal each academic year requires passing the previous year.
